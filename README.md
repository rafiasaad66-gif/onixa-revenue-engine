ONIXA — Revenue Intelligence & Client Acquisition Engine

An evidence-based client acquisition system designed to help ONIXA identify, research, qualify, and convert high-value business opportunities.

Mission

Build a repeatable acquisition pipeline:

Research → Verification → Diagnosis → Opportunity → Solution → Outreach → Sales → Revenue

The system does not rely on random outreach or generic sales messages. It researches each business, identifies observable friction in its digital customer journey, and develops a relevant solution.

Core Capabilities

- Market and niche discovery
- Business verification
- Public digital presence analysis
- Customer journey diagnosis
- Evidence-based opportunity scoring
- Tailored solution development
- Personalized outreach preparation
- Sales conversation preparation
- CRM and follow-up management
- Performance measurement and optimization

Operating Principles

1. No selling before understanding.
2. No outreach without research.
3. No diagnosis without evidence.
4. No solution without a diagnosed problem.
5. No unsupported financial claims.
6. Human approval before external actions.
7. Respect privacy and access restrictions.
